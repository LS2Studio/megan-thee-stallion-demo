Moulin Rouge!
The Musical

Megan made history on Broadway. The first female-identifying performer to play Zidler in any production of Moulin Rouge! The Musical worldwide.

🎭 Broadway Debut

Zidler
Al Hirschfeld Theatre

Megan took on the traditionally male role of Zidler, the flamboyant impresario of the Moulin Rouge nightclub. She stepped into the role created by Tony winner Danny Burstein, following Bob the Drag Queen.

Debut
March 24, 2026
Final Performance
May 1, 2026
Theatre
Al Hirschfeld Theatre, NYC
Historic First
First woman to play Zidler

Megan's run was originally scheduled through May 17, but she departed early on May 1. She was hospitalized mid-show on March 31 but returned to the stage on April 2. The production closed on August 30, 2026, after a seven-year run and over 2,200 performances.
